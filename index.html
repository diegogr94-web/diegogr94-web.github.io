Diego García Rincón

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Classics at the University of Toronto, where I also teach in the Department of Philosophy. I received my PhD from LMU Munich in 2025.

I work on ancient Greek and Roman philosophy, especially Plato’s metaphysics and political thought. My current project, Sages and Kings, examines how imperial-age Stoics and Platonists understood the knowledge needed to rule.
